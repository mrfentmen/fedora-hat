## The name, checked (2026-10-18)

Searched on GitHub (API search, plain and quoted terms):

  libraries sit there. Nobody finds us by accident; that is fine.

  installer plus cited federation — did **not** appear in those hits. It is a small slot
  in a big ocean; the claim stays only that the specific combination is not there, not
  that the word is free.

## What this repo does NOT claim

- It does not replace government push alerts (USGS WaterAlert and friends remain the full
  and authoritative versions; the crown only covers air + quakes + named-gauge flood).
- It does not "end ACP fragmentation" or any such framing. It spawns siblings and cites
  them; that is all federation ever promised here.
- The watchd file format is written to be obeyed by the watcher runtime in the sibling
  repo; nothing claims a super-set of what watchd checks today.
